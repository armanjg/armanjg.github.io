01 / About

Mathematics with a statistical lens.

I am a PhD student in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Washington. My interests center on statistical machine learning and the mathematical ideas that make modern inference possible.

I received a BSc in Mathematics and an MSc in Statistics. My master's thesis, supervised by Dr. Jingjing Wu at the University of Calgary, studied binary classification of high-dimensional data using Quadratic Discriminant Analysis.

02 / Research

Inference in complex, high-dimensional settings.

Current work spans synthetic-data inference, high-dimensional classification, and probability. The emphasis is on understanding when statistical procedures remain reliable as dimension, model complexity, and data-generation mechanisms change.

R1
Ongoing work

Statistical Inference for Generalized Linear Models Based on Synthetic Data

Methodological work on valid inference when observed data are augmented or replaced by synthetic samples generated under complex distributional mechanisms.

R2
Ongoing work

Phase Diagram of QDA for High-Dimensional Gaussian Mixtures

Asymptotic analysis of classification regimes under sparse, weak signals and high-dimensional covariance structure.

R3
Expository research

Topics in High-Dimensional Probability

Functional inequalities, concentration of measure, geometry, and probabilistic structure in high dimensions.

R4
MSc thesis

Binary Classification of High-Dimensional Data Using QDA

Theoretical phase diagrams for successful and impossible classification under progressively richer Gaussian mixture models.
